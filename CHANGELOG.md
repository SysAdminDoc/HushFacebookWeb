# Changelog

## Unreleased

* Checked in Violentmonkey on Chrome as well as Tampermonkey. The script starts in the page, takes Marketplace and search ads out before Facebook sees them, gives Copy link the post's own address and keeps a story view to yourself, the same as in Tampermonkey.
* The tests now run against scrubbed copies of what Facebook really sends: a feed page, the home page's inline feed data, a Marketplace page with an ad card, a search page with an ad, and the request that marks a story as seen. A new script turns a raw capture into one of these fixtures, replacing ids, names, places, text, tokens and links with stand-ins keyed by a private key, and a test checks every fixture for leftover ids, tokens, places and addresses.
* Groundwork for hiding posts on the page itself, for the features that can't catch a post in Facebook's data. It never marks the feed's own rows, which is what stalls scrolling in other blockers. Diagnostics counts hidden posts by reason. There's also a new General setting that leaves a one-line "Hidden: Sponsored. Show" note where a post was, and clicking it brings the post back. A post hidden by two switches at once stays hidden until both are off.
* Search ads and Marketplace ad cards have their own switches now, like on Android, and there's a new Hide sponsored reels switch that drops the ad breaks Facebook schedules inside videos and reels. All three are on by default. Every ad rule lives in one table that says where it was seen and what it removes.
* Checked the rest of the share sheet with Sanitize sharing links on. Share to WhatsApp gets the post's own address too, and sharing through Messenger, to a group or to a friend's profile works the same as without the script.
* Hide sponsored posts has a second layer. If an ad still gets drawn, the script reads the post's header the way you see it and hides the post when it says Sponsored or Ad. It sees through the ways Facebook disguises that label, like letters that are shuffled and put back in place by the layout with hidden decoys mixed in, or a label that points at text Facebook deletes a moment later. A post with only a Shop now style button isn't hidden, since group posts and shares carry those too. This layer works even when your manager runs the script as a content script, so Hide sponsored posts no longer switches off there. English labels only for now. Diagnostics shows how many ads each layer caught.
* Facebook Feed Filter's MIT license notice is in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt and at the end of the built script, since the page-side ad check follows its approach.
* New Hide Stories tray switch. It takes the Stories row off the top of Home, along with the "What's on your mind?" box unless you turn that part off, and the first post moves up into the space. It's off by default.
* New Hide suggested posts switch, on by default. Posts from Pages, people and groups you don't follow come out of Home and your groups feed before Facebook draws them, and any that slip through, or all of them in content mode, are hidden by the Follow or Join button next to the name. Search results, a Page's own posts and groups you're browsing aren't touched.
* New Hide reel rows in the feed switch for the rows of reels Facebook puts between posts. Reels your friends post stay. It's off by default.
* New Hide posts by words switch. List words, phrases, people, Pages, groups or sites, one per line, and posts on Home and in your groups feed that match are gone before Facebook draws them. A phrase only matches whole words, so "cat" leaves "concatenate" alone. Start a line with re: to write a pattern, or with "# " for a note to yourself. There are also switches that hide every photo, video or link post and every post with a colored background. A post opened from its link is never hidden, and comments don't count toward a match. In content mode posts are hidden on the page, with a note saying which line caught them. Diagnostics counts what each line hid and points out lines that aren't valid patterns.
* New Hide seen posts switch. Posts on Home and in your groups feed that you've already scrolled past stay away for the days you pick: 1, 3, 7 or 30. A post counts as seen once it's been on screen for a second, and the ones you see during a visit stay where they are until you come back. Facebook's page doesn't say which post is which any more, so the script knows a post by its photo or video, or by its first words, and keeps that list in your userscript manager. The switch's card has two buttons, one to show seen posts again in the tab you're in and one to forget the whole list.
* New Hide AI-detected posts switch, off by default like on Android. Posts Facebook labels as made with AI, and shares of them, come out of Home, your groups feed and search results before Facebook draws them. It goes by Facebook's AI info label, whether Facebook detected the AI or the author added the label. A post opened from its link and anything on a profile stay. It needs the script running in the page.
* New download switches for photos, videos, reels and stories, like on Android. Point at one and a Download button shows in its corner. Photos save the largest copy Facebook sent, and videos, reels and stories save the HD file unless you pick smaller files in the switch's card. Files get readable names like "Pat Example reel 1234 HD.mp4". When Facebook only streams a video in small pieces, you get a message saying there's no single file to save instead of a broken download. Download any video starts off, like on Android, and the other three start on. Photos work in content mode too. The rest need the script running in the page. If your manager won't save a file type, the script fetches the file and hands it to your browser instead. The button stays off anything a dialog, menu or chat window covers, and it saves whatever is under it at the moment you click, so a story that moved on saves the card you're looking at. While a file saves, its button reads Saving and won't start a second copy. If you cancel the save in your browser, you get a short note saying so and nothing else is tried.
* New Hide read receipts and Hide typing indicator switches for Facebook's chats, both off by default. The script edits what Facebook's chat connection sends, taking out the part that marks a chat read and turning "typing" into "not typing", and everything else in the message goes out exactly as it was. They can't reach end-to-end encrypted chats, which most one-to-one chats are now, and both switches say so. Diagnostics shows how many receipts and typing signals were held back.
* New Default comment order and Show every reply switches under Comments, both off by default. Default comment order opens a post's comments as Newest, or as All comments if you'd rather, on the post's own page and when you open it from the feed. Facebook only changes the order through its own menu, so the script picks from that menu once per post, and if you change it back by hand it stays that way. It reads the menu's titles from Facebook's data, so it works in your language. Show every reply clicks the "View 3 replies" buttons in an open post as they show up and stops once there's nothing left. That one goes by English button text for now. Both work in content mode too, and the feed's own comment boxes are left alone.
* HushFacebook Web has its own icon now, the Hush family's blue H with a small globe for the web. Your userscript manager shows it next to the script, and it's carried inside the script itself, so the manager never has to fetch it. The README opens with a banner and has two screenshots of the settings panel, taken on facebook.com.
* Each Facebook account you sign in with keeps its own settings now, so two people sharing a browser don't trade switches. The first time the script sees an account, it starts from the settings you already had, so nothing gets lost in the update. Signed out, it uses those shared settings. Diagnostics says which ones are in use, and exported settings files now say which format they're in.
* Six new Appearance switches, all off by default, and all of them work in content mode too. Force dark mode keeps Facebook in its dark theme, menus and pop-ups included, and Facebook's own choice comes back when you switch it off. AMOLED black theme turns dark mode's grays pure black, leaving comment bubbles, menus and text boxes a shade lighter so you can still see their edges. Accent color puts one of seven colors, or your own #RRGGBB, in place of Facebook's blue in light and dark mode, icons included, while the Facebook logo and the Like reaction keep theirs. Use the system font draws Facebook in your computer's own interface font or in any installed font you name. Use your computer's emoji shows your system's emoji instead of Facebook's pictures of them, and copied text still carries the emoji. Turn off screen transitions makes menus and dialogs appear at once, though loading spinners keep turning. Every one of them changes the page the moment you flip it, without a reload.
* Four new switches to clear Facebook's furniture, all off by default and all working in content mode. Hide tabs takes the top bar tabs you pick away, with Video, Marketplace and Gaming chosen to start, and the Find friends button too, and the other tabs close up the space. Hide Menu promotions takes Ads Manager, Your Ad Activity, the Create menu's Ad, Gaming Video and Play games out of the left column and Facebook's menu. Hide Meta upsells does the same for Meta AI, Meta Quest, Ray-Ban Meta, WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger Kids. Hide tab badges takes the "(3)" off the browser tab's title and hides the red counts on the top bar and left column. Everything is found by where its link goes, not by its name, so it works in any language.
* New Custom CSS box under Appearance for your own rules. They go in after everything the script does, so they win a tie with its built-in styles, and they apply and go away as you flip the switch. It's the quick fix when Facebook moves something before a switch catches up. No rule can hide the settings panel itself, so you can always take one back out. If you paste CSS into Hide posts by words by mistake, you get a note saying where it belongs.
* New Settings in your account menu switch, on by default. The menu that opens from your profile picture at the top right gets a HushFacebook Web row just above Log out, and clicking it opens the settings. The row is a copy of Facebook's own, so it matches in light and dark mode, and the script finds the menu by its link to your profile rather than by its words, so it works in any language. Its card has a choice that hides the round button in the corner, since you don't need both. It works in content mode too.
* New Open on a chosen tab switch, off by default. When you start Facebook in a new tab, it opens on the page you pick instead of Home: Marketplace, Feeds with the newest posts first, Video, Friends, your groups feed, Notifications or the Menu. Only the first page of a visit moves, so Home is still there when you click it, a reload stays put, and a link into Facebook opens where it leads.
* New Marketplace only switch, off by default, for when you only use Facebook to buy and sell. Facebook opens on Marketplace, the logo and Home take you there too, and the top bar's other tabs and Find friends go away. Profiles, chats, notifications and your groups still open from their links. While it's on, Open on a chosen tab and Following feed on Home wait their turn. If Marketplace isn't open to your account and keeps sending you back, it stops trying after a few goes and shows Home. Both switches work in content mode.
* New Stop stories moving on switch, off by default. A video story stays on screen when it ends instead of jumping to the next one, and you go on with the arrow when you're ready. Its card has a choice to play the story again instead. Photo stories still move on by themselves for now. It works in content mode too.
* Facebook started hiding an invisible joining mark after every letter of the "Ad" label, which got past the page-side ad check. It strips those marks now, along with the other invisible filler characters. With the script in content mode, a search page's two ads were hidden and its other results left alone.
* Hidden posts and rows can now be any block in the feed, not only posts.
* Facebook's home.php address counts as Home now, so Home-only features work there too.
* Releases now come with a SHA256SUMS.txt, so you can check that the script you downloaded is the one that was built.

## v0.0.1 (2026-10-10)

* The groundwork. Settings live in your userscript manager and get their own panel, which can back them up and show diagnostics. Underneath, a data layer cleans Facebook's feed as it loads and can hold back single requests.
* First features: Hide sponsored posts, View stories anonymously, Sanitize sharing links, Skip Facebook's link redirect, Following feed on Home, Tap to play and Turn off HDR brightness. All seven have run on facebook.com, and the script has been checked installed in Tampermonkey on Chrome.
* If two copies of the script are installed, the second one stays out of the way and the first one says so.
* Hide sponsored posts follows how Facebook marks ads as of October 2026. It now renames its ad fields and scrambles the post category, so the old markers would have missed every ad. The same switch also covers Marketplace ad cards and ads in search results.
* Copy link gives the post's own address. Without it, Facebook hands out a facebook.com/share link that leads back to whoever copied it.
* Tap to play starts a video only when you click that video. Clicking anywhere else, like the Reels link, no longer lets the next one start by itself.
* The settings switch at the top of the panel now reads on while the script is running.
